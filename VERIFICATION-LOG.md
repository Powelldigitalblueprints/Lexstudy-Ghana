# Verification log — cited briefs

Rule (from V0.3-PLAN.md): no brief is published until its
citation, court and date are checked against at least one public
source. Anything unverified stays out. Summaries are written in
our own words; no judgment text and no Legum or Dennislaw content
is copied. This log records what was checked, where, and when,
plus candidates that were considered but not used.
