# LexStudy Ghana — v0.3 plan (Research layer + study tools)

Status: PLAN ONLY — not built, not pushed, not deployed. Written
2026-11-13 after v0.2.1 went live with PostgreSQL in the second
Render account. Abdul chose "Plan v0.3" as the next step.

## Where v0.2.1 stands (verified)

- Live at lexstudy-ghana-zdt9.onrender.com, PostgreSQL-backed,
  accounts survive redeploys (proven 2026-11-13).
- Content: 8 subjects (5 topics each), 3 verified brief summaries
  (all Constitutional Law), 3 quizzes (5 questions each), 3 IRAC
  scenarios, 4 clearly-labelled hypothetical practice briefs.
- NOT yet built: payments, prices, paid restrictions, flashcards,
  timed mocks, citation search, legislation library, practice
  tracks, forum, full offline notes.

## Goal of v0.3

Add the Research layer and the study tools the Legum deep look
(2026-10-29) found missing from Legum's public pages: verified
cited briefs across more subjects, spaced-repetition flashcards,
timed mocks, and citation/topic search. Content depth is still the
gap — Legum has ~21 LLB courses with dozens of notes each — so
v0.3 grows depth in a verified, sustainable way rather than
claiming parity.

## Slice A — Verified briefs expansion (content)

- Target: from 3 to 12 verified brief summaries, spreading beyond
  Constitutional Law: Contract (2), Tort (2), Criminal Law (2),
  Land Law (1), Equity and Trusts (1), Constitutional Law (1 more).
- Topics, not pre-named cases: e.g. Contract formation
  (offer/acceptance), Tort duty of care, Criminal actus reus /
  mens rea, Land customary tenure, Equity trusts. Specific
  judgments are chosen ONLY after verification (see rule below).
- Every brief keeps the v0.2 format: citation, court, date,
  facts, issue, ratio, sources-checked list, and the warning to
  check the reported judgment before citing in court work.
  Summaries only — never full judgments, never copied content.

### Verification rule (non-negotiable, carried from v0.2)

No brief is published until its citation, court and date are
checked against at least one public authoritative or reputable
source (e.g. Dennislaw preview, GLR citation listings, court /
university sources). Anything unverified stays out, and the
verification log (case, citation, sources checked, date) is kept
in this repo as VERIFICATION-LOG.md. If a candidate case cannot
be verified, it is dropped, not guessed.

## Slice B — Flashcards with spaced repetition (study tool)

- Decks per subject, seeded from existing notes and briefs
  (principles, definitions, case ratios).
- Self-graded review: Again / Good / Easy, with simple
  SM-2-style intervals and a "due today" queue.
- Progress saved per account in PostgreSQL
  (new table: flashcard_reviews).
- Works logged-out for a single session only; saving needs an
  account, like quizzes.

## Slice C — Timed mocks (study tool)

- Timed quiz mode built on the existing quiz engine: fixed
  question set, countdown timer, auto-submit at time-up, then
  score + per-question review with explanations.
- Results saved per account (new table: mock_attempts) and
  shown on the account page.
- First mocks: one per subject that already has a quiz
  (Constitutional, Contract, Tort), then more as question
  banks grow.

## Slice D — Citation and topic search (research tool)

- Brief index page with filters: subject/topic, court, and
  citation search (e.g. "GLR 637").
- Search page extended to cover verified briefs by citation
  and principle, not just subject notes.
- No full-judgment republication; link out to authoritative
  sources where publicly available.

## Stretch (only if slices A–D land cleanly)

- Legislation library starter: index of key 1992 Constitution
  provisions with study notes and links to official sources.
  No republishing of long statutory text without a rights
  check.

## Explicitly NOT in v0.3

Payments, prices, paid-content restrictions, professional
practice tracks (v0.4), AI tutor, forum/community, full offline
notes, legal-education reform tracker (v0.4), firm case
management (never — out of scope).

## Data model (additive only)

New PostgreSQL tables via CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS in db.py,
mirrored in the SQLite fallback schema: flashcard_reviews,
mock_attempts. Existing tables untouched. No migration step,
no data loss risk to existing accounts.
